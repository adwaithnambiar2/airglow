AIRGLOW - DRAW BEYOND THE SCREEN

1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Open the airglow folder.
3. Double-click start_windows.bat.
4. Keep the terminal open. Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:5005

No pip install or virtual environment is needed.
Python 3.9 or newer must be installed.

Mouse: click and drag on the canvas.
Camera: click Camera, allow webcam access, and pinch thumb + index to draw.
Release the pinch to lift the brush.

Export PNG saves your drawing. Refreshing loses unsaved artwork.
Ctrl+C in the terminal stops the app.

If double-clicking fails, open CMD in this folder and run:
py start.py

Read README.md for controls, troubleshooting, tests, and AI attribution.
